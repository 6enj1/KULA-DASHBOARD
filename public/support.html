Frequently Asked Questions

What is KULA?

KULA is a food waste reduction app that connects you with restaurants and stores selling surplus food at discounted prices. Save money while helping reduce food waste.

How do I place an order?

Browse available surprise bags near you, add one to your cart, and complete your purchase. You'll receive pickup instructions with your order confirmation.

How do I pick up my order?

After placing an order, go to the restaurant during the specified pickup window. Show your order confirmation at the counter to collect your bag.

Can I get a refund?

If a restaurant is unable to fulfil your order, you will receive a full refund. For other issues, please contact us at support@kulasave.co.za.

How do I delete my account?

You can request account deletion by emailing support@kulasave.co.za. We will process your request and delete all associated data.

Is my payment information safe?

Yes. All payments are processed securely through Yoco. We never store your card details on our servers.
